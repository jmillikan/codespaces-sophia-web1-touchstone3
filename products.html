North Star Bakery - Products

Our Famous Breads

North Star is known best for our loaves of freshly made bread, especially our signature pumpernickel loaf! Here is a selection of our loaves:

  • Whole wheat loaf
  • White loaf
  • Baguette
  • Pumpernickel loaf
  • Sourdough loaf, sliced
  • Cinnamon raisin loaf, sliced

Our loaves are ordinarily priced from $5-$7 depending on the day and loaf.

Pastries

North star offers a selection of wonderful pastries:

  • Croissants and chocolate croissants
  • Plain and glazed donuts
  • Chocolate chip cookies

Our pastries are priced at $3-$5. Look for end-of-the-day discounts!

Coffee

North Star serves drip and french press coffee sourced from Starstone Coffee. Available add-ins:

  • Whole milk, half and half
  • Soy milk and oat milk
  • White sugar, raw sugar, honey

Bottomless brew coffee is $3. A 16 oz french press is $4.
