North Star Bakery - About

History

North Star Bakery was summoned into existence by a great wizard with a blinding flash of light on precisely midnight of September 2nd, 1927! Since then, every day that the bakery is not in operation, a terrible curse befalls the first person to drive down main street (usually baldness).

Sourcing Philosophy

We use exclusively locally grown ingredients in our breads! Local milk, wheat, rye and spices are the foundation of our bakery. (Our coffee comes from a little further away, but everything is local somewhere!)
